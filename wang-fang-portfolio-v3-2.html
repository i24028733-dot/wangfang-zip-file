Project 1 · Portfolio project

Where Memories Reside: Discover the Hidden Soul of Shenzhen Old Town

Role: Solo, built the website independently · Date: 2026.04.17 · Tools: GitHub Pages, street photography, copywriting, QR code generator

Project Background

Shenzhen is widely known for skyscrapers and fast-paced modern development. Its old urban areas and alleyways carry authentic local memories that are often overlooked. This project uncovers the grassroots, human-centred side of Shenzhen through a casual walking guide of old-town streets.

Creative Process

  1. Researched old-town streets and collected documentary street photography of daily urban life.
  2. Planned content and wrote descriptive copy focused on local atmosphere rather than travel tips.
  3. Designed muted layouts and typefaces to match the raw, realistic feeling of the photos.
  4. Built a scrolling single-page website on GitHub Pages (cover, introduction, street recommendations) and generated a QR code linking to it.

Final Outcome

An immersive web-based walking guide hosted on GitHub Pages. Viewers scan the QR code to open the site and explore the forgotten human charm of Shenzhen's old neighbourhoods.

QR: scan the code above to visit the project page.

Reflection

Done well: chose a less-popular perspective; visuals and tone create a consistent, gentle atmosphere; QR code makes online work easy to reach from a static PDF.
To improve: add interactive elements such as location markers or short resident stories; more diverse shooting angles.

Project 2 · Portfolio project

Negeri Sembilan Fencing Association Instagram Account Operation

My Role: Edit together with two partners · Platform: Instagram · Date: 2026.07.20 · Tools: Lightroom, Premiere Pro

Project Background

The Negeri Sembilan Fencing Association needed an official Instagram page to share competitions, athlete updates and behind-the-scenes content. It targets sports fans, parents and partners across Malaysia.

Creative Process

  1. Shot live competition footage and behind-the-scenes documentary photos at the fencing arena.
  2. Edited photos in Lightroom and cut highlight reels in Premiere Pro for Instagram feed and Reels.
  3. Wrote English captions, added hashtags, published posts, and adjusted direction from likes and comments.

Final Outcome

A fully operated Instagram account with steady follower growth, top competition reels reaching over 110 likes, and more than 700 new followers, strengthening the association's brand in the local sports community.

Reflection

Done well: learned the full workflow of overseas social media content, from shooting and editing to community management; became familiar with Instagram algorithms and audience habits.
To improve: test more short, fast-paced Reel formats to attract younger viewers.

Project 3 · Portfolio project

WeChat Official Account, Chinese Students Association in Malaysia

My Role: Photography · Platform: WeChat Official Account · Date: 2025.11.18 · Tools: Photoshop

Project Background

This official WeChat account serves thousands of Chinese students studying in Malaysia, releasing event announcements and sharing cultural stories. The goal is engaging articles and visuals that unite students and strengthen community.

Creative Process

  1. Planned and wrote original event recap articles for film sharing, football tournaments and Spring Festival galas.
  2. Made custom covers and illustrations for each post under consistent visual rules.
  3. Typeset and published articles, tracked reading and sharing data, and refined later posts.

Original Source: https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/fsk8Gff8DvYcocYQK5dd5w

Final Outcome

22 original articles completed. Monthly reading volume rose by 28%. Featured football and Spring Festival posts gained over 1,200 views. Reusable cover and article templates now simplify future production.

Reflection

Done well: turned offline student activities into online content for Chinese readers; learned to deliver cross-cultural student events online.
To improve: add interactive elements such as embedded short videos and photo galleries.
